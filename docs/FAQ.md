Q: Is this vibecoded? 
A: While I have made extensive use of LLMs over the course of this project, and there are certainly individual functions or widgets here and there that are very blatantly vibecoded, I would not at all say that this project is vibecoded as a whole. I am a mathematician who was employed as a computer science professor right as the AI boom was starting. The development of this software has been a learning experience for me as I transition into a new professional role.

Because of this, the main role of LLMs in this process has been to assist me in that learning process. The things I tended to use it for most were:
1. Detecting bugs (by far the biggest).
2. Alerting me to the right packages or tools that I would need for whatever I was trying to implement or whatever problems I was trying to solve, and getting me started when using them (for example, threading and multiprocessing).
3. Helping me structure my project in a professional and logical manner.

Despite having vibecoded pockets, this app is something I have worked on very carefully, with very specific intentions and in a very opinionated manner. It's something I was thinking about making for years before actually starting it, with several failed attempts prior. The reason I can be certain isn't vibecoded, I guess, is that I feel that I understand how the software works, both in a broad high level sense and in a low level particular sense.
